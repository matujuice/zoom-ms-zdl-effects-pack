# Owner decisions per effect

Moved from CLAUDE.md. Update when behaviour changes.


- Choral (rewritten 2026-10-07, Luca): synths only, never drums; the old formant filter on the input sounded different on every synth and its auto-level pumped and clicked, so it now tracks the input's pitch and sings it with its own saw voices through 3 formants per section. Same name and fxid 486. Knobs, less technical and more characterful: Choir (who sings), Size (how many), Chord (how they sing together), Sing (what they sing; the vowels live here, no Vowel knob), Pace, Feel, Glide, Tempo, Mix (Mix is the old Voice idea). Step 1 had Chord UNIS/OCT and Sing AAH/OOH/OOAH; Luca then asked for all settings at once (2026-10-08): Choir 17 (every section combination, MONKS, GOSPL), Chord 25 (UNIS, semitone steps +1..+11 up to OCT as Luca asked, labelled in semitones because interval names were unclear, 11 chords, DRONE), Sing 14 (six vowels incl. MMM, OOAH, VOWL, LA, DOO, HA, SWELL, CANON as a real round, WHSPR). Made for one oscillator per note; two detuned oscillators are not a use case and may jump octaves (Luca, 2026-10-08). Glide 0 = every note re-attacks (old note hushed on a legato change), Glide 1..100 = legato slides (Luca, 2026-10-08). Later ideas: stereo choir, a hall on the choir.
- DualShft: tempo-synced LFO on two pitch voices; Dly1/Dly2 free 12 ms..1 s, 101..112 synced note values 1/32..1bar (ring 3.9 s, 688 KB; a synced time too long is halved; Luca 2026-10-05).
- DubSiren: siren into a tape-style echo; Trig Hold / Pulse / SHold / SPuls (the S ones start on the next beat of a clock re-anchored by twin flips and never reset the LFO; Luca chose next beat over next bar, 2026-10-05); Rate 101..112 sync to the Tempo knob, Fast = 2x and Slow = 0.5x of the note value; echo Time is never synced; Fdbk 0 = echo off, default 70; siren level is 0.3 x full-scale at Vol 100.
- EuGate: Steps 1..64 and Notes 1..64 (polymeters); Shift delays the pattern; Reset OFF / NOTE / PEDAL / SYNC (SYNC = OFF, named for bar sync users, Luca 2026-10-05); Gap puts a small silence before a touching note; Mix stays on every effect; the cover is a 16-dot Euclid ring (round on the device), ghost "CLIDIAN", "5/16=3.3.3.3.4".
- WaveFold: Buchla 259 fold curve; the level match follows the input directly (it used to creep back slowly after a quiet decay, which made notes swell in).
- S.GN_L ("bad signal"): packets of 1..500 ms, lost by a Gilbert-Elliott model (Loss = share, Burst = outage length); Fill GAP/REPT/FADE/NOISE/REVRS/GARBL/LATE/RND (RND picks one per outage, never GARBL: too harsh, Luca); one Codec knob for spectral holes, low-pass, rate hold and bits; Jump = sudden quality drops; Line HIFI/VOIP/PHONE/WALKY kept as its own knob; Size free (no tempo sync). Cover: wide glitched S.GN_L with dotted ghost I and A, damage leaking into the knob labels. Concept notes: /mnt/project-files/ideas/bad-signal.md.
- Scrub: 7.9 s 16-bit buffer in the arena (348000 samples, 696 KB of the >= 705536 bytes; Luca asked for the most the arena holds, 2026-10-03; cleared 2048 samples per block after loading); Pos scrubs a read head over the last 6 s in 600 steps of 10 ms (0 = 6 s ago, 600 = now, label "0ms" (not NOW); read as raw x 100, not through the 3.05 guess; Luca dropped the Range knob 2026-10-03 for this), Glide (the brief called it Smooth; 5-letter names like the rest of the pack) glides it; grains always play at normal speed, so the pitch never bends (Luca chose constant pitch over pushing running grains with the head, 2026-10-03); the freeze is a crossfaded loop (new voice fades in over the first quarter of each Grain while the old fades out), not a 50 % overlap cloud, which beat on held tones; Dir FWD / REV / PING / RAND (ping-pong voices alternate and turn round on the same sample, so wherever a voice turns round (PING always, RAND on a coin flip) the seam crossfade is 1 ms instead of the quarter-grain one, which cancelled against its own mirror image); Grain 101..112 are note values synced to a Tempo knob (8th knob, 40..240 BPM), a long synced grain pulls the head in to fit the buffer; Spray scatters grains either side of the head, up to +-250 ms, label "+-N" in ms (Luca, 2026-10-03); Rec LIVE / HOLD / STOMP (STOMP records while switched off, relies on the pedal passing audio to a bypassed effect: unverified). Concept: /mnt/project-files/ideas/effect-ideas.md.
- DIRTBOX redesign (2026-10-06, Luca: a dedicated distortion box for an acid machine): models TS9, DIST+, DS-1, RAT2, MUFF, SFUZZ, MT-2 ordered least to most aggressive (the RAT became the RAT 2; Super-Fuzz added on request); each follows the real circuit and professional digital models; 3-band acid EQ (110 Hz shelf, 1 kHz peak, 4.5 kHz shelf, +-12 dB) after the pedal; defaults RAT2, Tone 80, EQ flat; ZNR retuned for 303 lines (30 ms hold, ~80 ms close). Briefly renamed ACIDBOX, then back to DIRTBOX the same day because Acidbox is an Erica Synths product (Luca picked DIRTBOX). Earlier DirtBox history: DirtBox (2026-10-05, Luca asked for a few distortion models in one effect plus an automatic ZNR; details below are Claude's first design, not yet heard on the pedal): Model DS-1 / RAT / METAL on one knob (DS-1 was ACID until Luca renamed it, 2026-10-05); DS-1 = the built-in distortion of the Behringer TD-3 (Luca: "the distortion modeling of the synths internal distortion", for the MeeBlip triode, acid lines, drones), which is a Boss DS-1 copy; ZNR detects on the clean input, measures the noise floor itself (capped at -60 dBFS), knob = margin above it, 1:3 expander with 50 ms hold and ~150 ms release so kick tails fade naturally; wet path only; models rebuilt from circuit analyses and digital recreations (Luca: models "sound slightly off, ... follow those", 2026-10-05), so Drive = the pedal's Dist knob and DS-1/METAL distort even at 0; makeup keeps the wet peak near the input at any Drive; no tempo, Mix is the DJ crossfade. Cover (Luca, 2026-10-05): DubSiren-style metal case, beaten up (dented, missing screws, rust; no jumper wires, Luca), thick even DIRTBOX title (3 px stems, 2 px bars) centred with slight wear, a few straight cracks and no specks ("broken but intentional and neat"); the big classic acid smiley is a sticker a modder stuck on later, wrapped round the case's left edge (wall still visible) and over the D's lower left corner, peeling a little, title still readable (Luca, 2026-10-05). ZNR made stronger (Luca 2026-10-07, "make znr more powerful"): margin +9..+30 dB (was +6..+24) and a 1:5 expander (was 1:3); noise alone drops ~77 dB at ZNR 50 (was ~29), note tails above -63 dBFS untouched.
- Tempo-knob sync reset (Luca, 2026-10-05): every Tempo knob (DualShft, Choral, EuGate, DubSiren, Scrub, and later Breather, SyncEQ, GridDly, Sweep) runs 0..441 and holds each BPM twice: 0..240 = BPM (below 40 = 40), 241..441 = twin copy, BPM = screen - 201, label shows the BPM on both. Flipping between a BPM and its twin restarts the LFO / pattern / grain with no tempo change, so a MIDI host can send one knob edit per downbeat; no extra knob. All five restart only on a flip; a tempo change only changes the speed (DualShft and Choral restarted on any Tempo change until Luca changed it, 2026-10-07, so long LFOs with Mozaic Every 8 aren't cut by a tempo change). Read Tempo as raw x 100 up to 4.41 (`*_tempo_ui`), never through the 3.05 guess. Tempo is the 8th knob on all five (Luca, 2026-10-05), so the Mozaic bar-sync script sends one knob number; keep it there on any new synced effect. Switched off, DualShft, Choral, EuGate and DubSiren leave the input untouched but keep their LFO / pattern / beat clock running and follow flips (Scrub restarts a grain on switch-on). docs/IPHONE-SYNC.md has the per-effect "settings for bar sync" table: keep it in step.
- Breather (Luca, 2026-10-05; first called Pump, renamed by Luca): two knobs, Targt DRY / VERB / BOTH / SEND (what moves) and Shape DUCK / GATE / RISE (how), all 12 pairs; no filter of any kind; no Mix (Depth and Verb set the amounts); Shift 0..100 % of a beat replaced a Pattern knob (labels +1/16, +1/8, +3/16, +1/4 at 25/50/75/100); GATE cuts on the downbeat and opens after Curve, like a hard DUCK (Luca 2026-10-06; it first opened on the downbeat); Tempo 8th, default 160; defaults are a sidechain pump on dry and reverb together (BOTH, DUCK, Depth 80, 1/4, Shift 0, Curve 50, Verb 35, Size 50; Luca 2026-10-06); Size folds tone in (bigger = darker). Switching on restarts on the one only when no twin flip came in ~8 s. fxid 493 (492 was the dropped Metro branch). Previews: /mnt/project-files/pump/preview/. Cover (Luca, 2026-10-06): BREATHER on an 8-step grid, one size; two kick envelopes run through the letters (sparse dots under the curve, ink above), curve dotted between letters, down arrows on the kicks and a dotted gain line under the title (DualShft style); no sinking letters. Rounds: /mnt/project-files/breather-cover/.
- Sweep (Luca, 2026-10-07): one effect with a Type knob instead of separate phaser, flanger and filter; Type has 8 named positions (PH 4, PH 8, FL +, FL -, LP, BP, HP, NTCH) instead of a Var knob whose meaning changes (a knob label can't see another knob, Luca asked for a rework); Rate synced from 8 bars up to 1/32 note, ordered slowest to fastest so the whole knob (free Hz first, then synced) goes faster all the way up, default 1 bar = 109 (Luca, after the pedal test 2026-10-07; first version had 1/4..8 bars fastest first); mono; Tone is feedback brightness on phaser/flanger and drive on the filters; Mix defaults to 100. A synced flip puts the LFO at (32 x bars mod length) / length, lengths in 32nd notes, so long sweeps survive the per-bar flips. fxid 496 (495 is GridDly). Cover f39bf87.
- GridDly (fxid 495, scoped 2026-10-06/07, /mnt/project-files/ideas/delay-scope.md): one delay, six engines on Type (DIGI TAPE DUB REVRS TAPS LOFI; Luca kept all six); "Ducked" became the Duck knob so every engine can duck; Luca removed the Hold (freeze) knob and asked for a Tail toggle like the factory delays (knob 9: ON = repeats ring out when switched off, OFF = they stop); 16-bit 7.9 s ring like Scrub so 1bar fits down to ~31 BPM and 2bar exists; first built as BarDelay (the pedal has a stock Delay); Luca renamed it GridDly 2026-10-07.
- Every effect except DubSiren, Breather and SyncEQ has a Mix knob, and it is a DJ-style crossfade: dry gain min(1, 2 - 2m), wet gain min(1, 2m), so both are at full level at 50. DubSiren adds its siren on top of the untouched input, so it has Vol instead; Breather's Depth and Verb set the amounts; SyncEQ is an EQ with Level.
- Bar tag and SyncEQ (Luca, 2026-10-06, thread "Slots 4-6 tempo sync"): Mozaic can only edit slots 1-3, so synced effects there pass the bar on through the Dry buffer right half (src/custom/common/drytag.h, docs/TEMPO-SYNC.md "Bar tag"). Every tempo effect sends and receives; effects without Tempo pass it on and never write ctx[4]. They can't start it, so SyncEQ (fxid 494) is a sender with EQ and tone shaping (LoCut, Low, Mid, MidF, High, HiCut, Drive, Tempo 8th, Level; no Mix, it's an EQ), only needed when no tempo effect sits in slots 1-3. Only an effect with Tempo on FOLLOW (bottom of the knob, screen 0..39, label FOLLW) follows the tag, bars and BPM (120 before any tag, then the last BPM is held if the sender goes away, Luca 2026-10-06); on a BPM it ignores the tag and runs free or on its own resets (Luca: "they shouldn't always blindly follow"). In slots 1-3, FOLLOW needs Mozaic's Send knob on another slot. Mozaic script v3 has a Send knob (OFF, Slot 1..3) instead of v2's three slot pads. Passed on the MS-60B 2026-10-07 (SyncEQ, EuGate, DubSiren, Scrub on FOLLW).

## Restored word for word from the pre-#20 CLAUDE.md

- EuGate: Reset SYNC = OFF (named for bar sync users, Luca 2026-10-05). EuGate builds its Euclidean pattern as a 64-bit mask by adding `Notes` and subtracting `Steps` on overflow (no integer division or modulo).
- WaveFold: the level match uses `ein * rsqrt(ein * elp)` instead of a divide.
- No float-to-unsigned casts: the linker can't resolve `__c6xabi_fixfu`. Write `(unsigned int)(int)(x)`. (This broke the EuGate build once.)
- S.GN_L: `effect_name` is SGNL (file SGNL.ZDL, symbols, SONAME); the manifest's `display_name` S.GN_L goes only into the descriptor name entry (LinkerConfig.display_name).
- TempoPrb (fxid 499, `src/probes/`) is a hardware probe, never released; it tests whether a custom effect can follow the pedal's tempo the TAPEECH3 way. `build_all.py` builds one only when named.
